# Seasonal direction: a creative brief for the booker

Historical proposal. Superseded by the implemented Rise to Supremacy career ladder and seasonal strategies in 7.8; see README.md.

## What exists today

The Office tracks three optional achievements each twelve-week season: keep a promise, deliver a story payoff, and earn a 75-rated show. The season report records shows, average rating, kept promises, and payoffs. Careers continue indefinitely. Cash, reputation, audience satisfaction, trust, stories, and the opening Sweeps arc provide shorter-term direction. There is no final victory or required seasonal destination.

## Recommended approach

At the start of a twelve-week season, choose a creative brief, or stay in free booking. Pick one headline ambition and up to two supporting goals. Select the wrestlers and tone yourself. Offer several ways to make progress so no goal dictates winners, props, match rules, or exact cards.

Suggested briefs:

| Brief | Possible routes | Example season ending |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Make someone matter | Feature a chosen wrestler across distinct opponents; build their trust; give them consequential story beats | A retrospective about their rise, resilience, or reinvention—even if they lost the final match |
| Give the town a story | Build a rivalry, answer a challenge, deliver an aftermath | A monthly-show conclusion followed by a reaction from both wrestlers |
| Build a locker room | Keep promises, develop a team, spotlight underused talent | A roster-led celebration with the people you invested in |
| Find the promotion’s identity | Satisfy a chosen audience through varied cards, develop signature presentations | A season poster celebrating the style the promotion established |
| Keep the lights on | Finish several shows profitably, maintain a chosen reserve, or recover from an early deficit | Bev’s report on what the promotion can afford next season |

Example: “Make Sweeps matter.” Count meaningful appearances against three distinct opponents, an answered promise, and either a story conclusion or sustained trust growth. A clean sweep of wins is unnecessary. A memorable losing effort and a new partnership can count toward the same ambition.

## Progress without a prescription

Show the brief and progress on the booking desk, with explanations in the recap: “Sweeps faced a new rival; two of three distinct opponents featured.” Suggestions should name opportunities, not mark creative alternatives as incorrect. Completed shows earn credit; draft cards and repeated clicks do not. Replaying the same beat cannot farm progress. Imported history without detailed events should say progress is unavailable rather than invent credit.

Let players revise the brief at any time. Keep the old brief in the season journal, retain credit for applicable completed actions, and explain what changed. No penalty, mandatory conversation, or additional booking restriction. A late-season choice should offer an achievable shorter ambition. Avoid scaling targets from stats that players can manipulate immediately before choosing.

Use recognition rather than power rewards: a season headline, a poster, wrestler-specific closing dialogue, and an archive of the cards that made it happen. Partial completion gets a distinctive retrospective too. No stat boosts, cash penalties, or loss of access for leaving a goal unfinished.

## First version worth building

Start with three briefs: Make someone matter, Give the town a story, and Build a locker room. Reuse the existing twelve-week reports, promises, story incidents, and show participants. Add explicit event records for meaningful character beats and a baseline snapshot when choosing a brief. Save the chosen goals, credited event IDs, revisions, and final report so reloads and imports cannot award progress twice.

Decisions to discuss: should one brief be the default or should free booking be the default? Should the season finale be week twelve’s existing tour show? Should success be a simple completion stamp or a prose assessment with several possible endings? My recommendation: free booking until a brief is selected, the existing week-twelve show as the finale, and prose endings supported by transparent counters.
